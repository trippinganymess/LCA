## 🌻SunChillFlower: LeetCode Accountability Dashboard

SunChillFlower is a minimalist, web-based accountability platform designed to turn the solitary grind of LeetCode into a shared journey. By linking your progress with a partner, this tool ensures that "chill" time is earned through consistent problem-solving.

🔗 [sunchillflower.me](sunchillflower.me)

### 🚀 The Concept

The "SunChill" philosophy is simple: Consistency over Intensity.
Instead of complex charts and deep metrics, this dashboard focuses on the most important metric: Did you show up today? It serves as a shared visual space where you and your accountability partner can monitor streaks, daily completions, and "Sun" status (active progress).

### ✨ Features

Real-time Progress Tracking: Direct integration with LeetCode profiles to fetch your daily "Solved" count.

Minimalist UI: A distraction-free dashboard that highlights your current streak and your partner's status.

Accountability Sync: Designed specifically for 1-on-1 or small group pairings to ensure no one falls behind.


### 🛠 Setup for Partners

To get started with your accountability partner on SunChillFlower:
- Visit the App: Navigate to sunchillflower.me.
- Share your Link: Send your unique dashboard URL to your partner.
- Set the Rules: Decide on a daily minimum (e.g., "1 Medium per day" or "2 Easy per day") and let the tracker do the rest.

### 📈 Why use this?

No Manual Logging: No more texting "I'm done" or updating spreadsheets.
Visual Pressure: Seeing your partner's "Sun" shining makes you want to get yours too.
Built for Longevity: The clean aesthetic prevents "dashboard burnout."
